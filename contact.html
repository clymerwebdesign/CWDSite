FAQ

Common questions

How long does it take to build my website?

Most sites are finished within 5 to 7 days of receiving everything I need from you. Larger projects may take up to two weeks. The biggest factor is how quickly you can get me your content, like photos and any text you want on the site.

What do you need from me to get started?

Your logo if you have one, any photos you want used, and a general idea of what pages you need. If you don't have photos or a logo yet, we can work around it.

What happens if I don't like what you build?

Every project includes one round of revisions after I deliver the first draft. You send me a list of changes and I make them. Additional revision rounds beyond that carry a small fee.

Will my website work on phones?

Yes, every site I build is fully mobile-friendly. Most of your customers are going to find you on their phone, so this is non-negotiable.

Can I update the website myself after it's done?

No, but that's what I'm here for. Reach out anytime you need something changed and I'll take care of it.

What is a custom domain and do I need one?

A custom domain is your website's address, like yourbusiness.com. Without one, your site has a generic web address that looks unprofessional. Every package I offer includes custom domain setup, and I handle the whole process for you so you don't have to figure anything out on your own.

How do I get in touch with you?

You can email me at clymerwebdesign@gmail.com or reach out on Instagram. I typically respond within a day.
